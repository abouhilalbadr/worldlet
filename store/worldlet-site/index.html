AI scenes

Describe a place, and your AI assistant builds it

  1. Copy the Worldlet prompt from Settings.
  2. Describe your scene to ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, like "a Moroccan riad with a fountain and orange trees".
  3. Paste the reply back into Worldlet. It checks the scene and draws it in your sky.

Scenes are plain data that Worldlet checks before drawing, so nothing from the internet ever runs as code. No AI runs on our side either; you use the assistant you already have.

{
  "worldlet": 1,
  "name": "Riad courtyard",
  "ground": { "type": "plain", "color": "#c9a57a" },
  "objects": [
    { "shape": "cylinder", "radius": 1.6,
      "position": [0, 0.85, -1], "color": "#f2ece0" },
    { "shape": "sphere", "radius": 0.32,
      "color": "#7fd0de",
      "animate": { "type": "bob" } },
    { "shape": "pyramid", "glow": true,
      "repeat": { "pattern": "ring", "count": 6 } }
  ]
}

Privacy

Private by design

No account, no ads, no analytics

There is nothing to sign up for and nothing tracking you.

Your things stay in your browser

Settings, links, tasks and notes are saved in Chrome. The developer never sees them.

Weather gets a rough location only

Worldlet sends your city's position, rounded to about 1 km, to Open-Meteo for the forecast.
