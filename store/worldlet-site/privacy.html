Privacy

Privacy policy

Effective 16 October 2026

The short version: Worldlet has no account, no server, no analytics and no ads. Everything you set up stays in your browser. The only things that leave it are the few requests needed to show the weather, listed below.

What stays in your browser

Your settings, quick links, name, logo, background photo, AI scene, tasks, notes and timer are saved with Chrome's own extension storage. The developer never receives them.

  • Settings and quick links use chrome.storage.sync. If you have Chrome sync turned on, Google copies them to your other computers as part of your Chrome profile.
  • Everything else (logo, photo, AI scene, tasks, notes, timer) uses chrome.storage.local and stays on this computer.
  • Site icons for your quick links come from Chrome's built-in icon cache. Worldlet does not download them.

Your position is only read if you choose "Use my location" and allow it in Chrome's prompt. You can always search for a city instead. These services receive requests directly from your browser, so they also see your IP address, as with any website. Their own policies apply: Open-Meteo terms and privacy and BigDataCloud privacy policy.

What Worldlet never does

  • It does not collect, sell or share your personal data, and the developer has no copy of it.
  • It does not track your browsing, read the pages you visit, or use analytics or advertising.
  • It does not run code downloaded from the internet. AI scenes are plain data files that Worldlet checks and draws with its own code.

The use of information received from Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Removing Worldlet from Chrome deletes everything it stored. To keep a copy first, use Settings → Your setup → Back up.
